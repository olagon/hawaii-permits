## Full description
Hawaiʻi Permits tells you exactly which permits, licenses, and reservations you need to hike, camp, hunt, fish, or visit outdoor places in Hawaiʻi. State parks, county beach parks, national parks, forest reserves, wildlife refuges, and private campgrounds, all in one place.

What Hawaiʻi Permits does:

* Search any place, with or without ʻokina and kahakō. Type "kaena" and find Kaʻena.
* See what each place needs: the permit, when it applies, how to get it, what it costs, and when booking opens.
* Plan a trip. Add places and dates and Hawaiʻi Permits builds one checklist for the whole trip, adds prerequisites like hunter education, and reminds you the moment a booking window opens.
* Keep your permits in a wallet on your phone so you can show a ranger even with no signal.
* Get weather and park alerts by island.
* Works offline. Download an island map pack and everything except live alerts works in airplane mode.
* Map of every place, colored by who manages the land, with closed and no access places clearly marked.

Hawaiʻi Permits is free and open source, made by volunteers at Kealoha Labs. It is not an official government app. When Hawaiʻi Permits and an official source disagree, the official source wins. Always confirm on the official page before you go.

No accounts. No tracking. No ads. Nothing you save leaves your device.

## App privacy answers (App Store) / Data safety (Play)
* Data collected: none.
* Data shared with third parties: none.
* Tracking: none.
* Location: used only on device to center the map when the user taps "Near me". Not stored, not sent.
* Photos and files: the permit wallet stores files the user picks, on device only.
* Notifications: local reminders scheduled on device.
* Account required: no.
* Ads: no.
* Encryption export compliance (iOS): uses only standard HTTPS. Answer "No" to the custom encryption question.
