# Code of Conduct

Hawaiʻi Permits is a community project. Be kind. Be patient. Be pono.

## We expect

* Respect for every person, including land owners, agency staff, and first time contributors.
* Respect for Hawaiian culture, place names, and cultural sites.
* Honest data. Cite your sources. Say when you are not sure.
* Plain, friendly words in issues and reviews.

## We do not allow

* Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
* Publishing locations of burial sites or other sensitive cultural places.
* Copying text or photos from sites that do not allow it.

## Reporting

Open an issue or email the maintainer through the address on the GitHub profile for `olagon`. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributors that break these rules.
